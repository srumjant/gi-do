# The Boss Show Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Batch 1 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-boss-show-design.md`: all six bosses react and talk, move with more life, make an entrance and lose in a funny way of their own, with no change to how a fight plays.

**Architecture:** A pure show (`src/game/bossShow.ts`) turns a boss's state, the events its view drained, the hero and the difficulty into a look and a line, once per rendered frame, advancing by the steps taken. A shared Phaser helper (`src/gfx/bossShow.ts`) draws the bubble, the banner and two overlays, and applies the look to the boss's pictures after its view placed them, undoing it after the scene renders so nothing accumulates. Pictures made by rule (`src/gfx/bossFrames.ts`) give eyes shut, white and red copies, Toomas's underpants and the Yeti's snowman. Each of the six views makes a show and plays its own defeat.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Phaser 4 (images, tweens, particles; the scene's RENDER event), Vitest. Commands run from `next/` in `.claude/worktrees/boss-show`.

## Task 4: The pure show

```ts
export interface ShowInput {
  boss: CourseBoss;
  /** The fx kinds the view drained this frame. */
  events: readonly string[];
  /** world.animFrame: the show advances by the steps since it last ran. */
  frame: number;
  heroDead: boolean;
  heroHasCape: boolean;
  /** Super Easy or Easy: the boss blurts out hints. */
  hints: boolean;
}
export interface BossLook {
  dy: number; sx: number; sy: number; angle: number;
  /** 0..1: the white overlay's alpha. */ flash: number;
  /** 0..1: the red overlay's alpha. */ red: number;
  eyesShut: boolean;
  /** 0..1: the name banner's alpha, 0 when gone. */ banner: number;
}
export function createBossShow(): BossShowState;
export function stepBossShow(s: BossShowState, input: ShowInput): { look: BossLook; line: string | null /* i18n key said now, or null */ };
```

Timings (frames): intro hop 30, banner 100 (fading over the last 20), line on screen 90, shrug lines at most every 180, hint after 480 without a hit then every 600, flash 12, hit squash 14, wobble 20, blink 6 every 200 (not while dizzy), crouch while winding up. Red = 0.35 × hearts lost / hearts. Priority when several happen in one frame: beaten > hit (or "OH EI!" when one heart is left) > laugh > shrug > hint > intro.

- [ ] Tests (`tests/bossShow.test.ts`), each driving `stepBossShow` with a built boss (`buildCourseBoss`) and hand-set fields: the intro on `wake` with the hop and the banner; a shrug line, then none for 180 frames however many more shrugs; a hit says `boss_hit_1`, the next `boss_hit_2`; the last heart says `boss_last`; the hero dying or losing the cape makes it laugh, once; no hint on Normal; a hint on Super Easy after 480 frames and the right key per boss and state (the T-Rex by round); a hit resets the hint clock; beaten says its line once, and the look is plain from then on; red grows with hearts lost; eyes shut while dizzy.
- [ ] Run, fail, write `src/game/bossShow.ts`, pass.

## Task 5: The Phaser show

`BossShowView(scene, kind)`, `update(world, boss, events, body, parts)` once per rendered frame after the view's own placing; it records the parts' x, y, scale and angle, applies the look, and on the scene's `RENDER` event puts them back. The bubble is a Graphics drawn once per line and a Text, following the boss's head; the banner is a big Text kept at the top of the camera's view; the overlays copy the body's transform and texture (`whiteKey`/`redKey` of its key) each frame; eyes shut swaps the body to `shutKey` while it lasts and back.

## Task 6: The six views

For each view: make `this.show = new BossShowView(scene, '<kind>')`; in `update`, keep the drained fx (`const fx = q.fx.splice(0)`), play them as now, then after `place` call `this.show.update(world, q, fx.map((f) => f.kind), body, parts)`. Defeats, in each view's `defeated` branch:

- Doll Queen: parasol flies off (as now); she spins once, then hops away to the nearer side with tear particles and fades.
- Pterodactyl: tumbles (angle 540) down to the floor in feathers, then flaps up and off.
- Vana Toomas: helmet, plate and shield fly off spinning; the body switches to the underpants and hops off the side.
- Pizza Chef: a poof, the chicken texture, clucks, runs off flapping.
- Yeti: slips (angle 90), slides along the floor, a poof, a snowman bounces in where he stopped.
- T-Rex: spins, shrinks to a quarter with confetti, bounces twice.

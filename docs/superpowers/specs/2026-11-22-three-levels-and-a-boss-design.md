# Three levels and a boss for every theme

**Date:** 2026-11-22
**Status:** Proposed. Supersedes the batch plan of `2026-11-22-extra-levels-design.md` (whose
batch 1, Doll Garden II and Dinosaur Canyon II, is built and merged); keeps everything it learned.

## Summary

Every theme becomes three hand-built levels that grow harder inside the theme's own setting,
and ends in a boss. The original levels are reworked as each theme's first level. The story
becomes one chase per theme: the theme's villain runs off with the sibling at the end of levels
1 and 2, and holds them in a cage at the end of level 3; beat the boss to free them, and the
next theme's villain snatches them again.

The owner's child calls the dark guards that bounce you back "zombies". They become zombies:
same rule, a proper zombie look, a groan, one kind per theme.

Built one theme at a time, the child playing each before the next. **Batch 1 is Doll Garden.**

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-22)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Story | Chase through levels 1-2, rescue at the boss in level 3 |
| Bosses | Designed here, all six |
| Zombies | The guards, as zombies: can't be stomped, push you back until shot; one kind per theme |
| First batch | One whole theme, Doll Garden |
| Original levels | Reworked as each theme's level 1 (the owner's earlier "don't change them" is lifted) |
| The final boss | Stays the T-Rex, made fun, and redrawn: today it does not read as a dinosaur |

The child plays Super Easy and sometimes harder. As before, the challenge on Super Easy comes
from puzzles and skill, and every obstacle is checked on all four difficulties.

## Each theme, three steps up

Each theme has one tool. Level 1 hands it over and uses it plainly; level 2 uses it to open
things; level 3 asks for it in harder combinations, then the boss is beaten with it. Tools from
earlier themes come back as reminders.

| Theme | Tool | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Doll Garden | bow | **Nukuaed**: the garden, the first zombies, the bow found | **Aiavärav** (Garden Gate): drawbridge, high target, zombie tunnel (today's Doll Garden II) | **Nukumaja** (Doll House): two targets for one gate, targets guarded by zombies | **Nukukuninganna**, the Doll Queen |
| Dinosaur Canyon | fart jump | the canyon, first cliff | the canyon wall, fart and bow together (today's Dinosaur Canyon II) | the volcano: climbs with lava below | **the Pterodactyl**: it circles high over the canyon, and only a fart jump reaches it |
| Tallinn | big head | the old town streets, cracked walls | the towers: springs up, walls down | the town hall square | **Vana Toomas**, the old town's knight: smash his armour |
| Palermo | chicken ray | the piazza | the market, a giant in the way | the cathedral steps | **the Pizza Chef**, guarded by giants |
| Winter | all four, plus ice | the snow field: slippery ice | the frozen lake | the ice palace | **the Yeti** |
| Toy Castle | everything | the gate | the halls | the throne room | **the T-Rex**, the final boss, redrawn and with a new fight |

Doll Garden, Dinosaur Canyon, Tallinn, Palermo, Winter and Toy Castle are designed in detail below; each later theme is designed when
its batch starts.

## Doll Garden, in detail

**Level 1: Nukuaed.** The original level, rebuilt by hand: open garden, steps and gentle pits,
`?` blocks, the rainbow block, the cat, dolls, cars and bats. Halfway through, a bow; just after
it, the first two zombie dolls, out in the open, where shooting them is the obvious thing. At
the end the Doll Queen stands with the sibling and runs off as you arrive.

**Level 2: Aiavärav.** Today's Doll Garden II with its guards turned into zombie dolls and its
ending turned into the chase: the spring still takes you up the tower, and the Queen leaps away
from the top with the sibling.

**Level 3: Nukumaja.** Inside the Doll House. New: a gate that needs **two** targets shot; a
target behind a row of zombies, so you clear them first; a target you can only line up from a
platform. Then a checkpoint, and the throne room.

**The boss: Nukukuninganna.** A doll three times the hero's height, holding a big parasol, with
the sibling in a cage behind her. The throne room's door shuts behind you.

- The parasol shields her: stomps and arrows bounce off.
- A target appears on a post somewhere in the room. Shoot it, and the parasol flies away; she
  sits down dizzy, with stars round her head, for a few seconds. Stomp her (or shoot her) then.
- Three hits. After each, she gets up quicker, throws more, and the next target is harder: low
  first, then high (jump and shoot), then across the room.
- She throws little hearts that drift slowly across the room, and calls two zombie dolls when
  she is hit.
- A bow station in the room comes back like every other station.
- Beaten, she bursts into a heap of toys, the cage opens, and you walk in to your sibling.

On Super Easy her hearts are slow, she waits longer between throws, and the cape every life
covers one mistake; on Hard she is quick.

## Dinosaur Canyon, in detail (batch 2)

Designed after Doll Garden was built and the owner said to carry on. The tool is the fart
jump; the bow comes along as a reminder, for zombie dinos. The villain carrying the sibling is
the Pterodactyl, flying, so the chase endings show her flying off with the sibling in her claws.

**Level 1: Dinosauruse kanjon.** The original level, rebuilt: canyon ledges and gentle pits,
dinos, bats, `?` blocks. The first cliff, too tall to jump, with the fart station right in
front of it. Then a bow and two zombie dinos, and a cannon on a ledge. At the end the
Pterodactyl, perched on a rock with the sibling, flies off as you arrive.

**Level 2: Kaljusein (the Canyon Wall).** Today's Dinosaur Canyon II: the two-step wall, the
ledge you fart up to so you can shoot the target, the cannons. Two zombie dinos join the
cannons, and the end becomes the chase: she waits on top of the mesa.

**Level 3: Vulkaan (the Volcano).** New: **lava**, orange and bubbling. Falling in is like a
pit (the cape throws you out once; without it, back to the flag). **Vents** in the lava spit a
fireball straight up every few seconds, glowing first so a child can time the jump past them.
Fart climbs with lava below, stepping stones, zombie dinos on ledges. Then the arena: the door
shuts behind you, and the Pterodactyl.

**The boss: the Pterodactyl.** She flies back and forth over the arena, 6.5 tiles up, the
sibling in a cage on a rock at the far end.

- No plain jump reaches her back, on any difficulty; a fart jump does, on every difficulty
  (Hard's fart jump clears 8.8 tiles). Two fart stations in the arena come back as always.
- Land on her back: a hit. Jumping up under her, you pass up through her and come down on
  her back, so a jump when she is overhead works (bouncing off her belly made it the one jump
  that never did, in the browser). Touch her side on the way down: you are pushed off, no harm,
  because she is the one to catch, not the one doing the hurting.
- She drops rocks straight down when she is over you, with a shadow on the ground first, slow
  on Super Easy. Rocks hurt; an arrow breaks one.
- Three hits. After each she screeches, flies up out of sight for a moment, calls two zombie
  dinos (never more than two up), and comes back faster and dropping more.
- Beaten, she tumbles down and bursts into feathers; the cage opens.

## Tallinn, in detail (batch 3)

The tool is the **big head**: it smashes cracked stone, and it is the only thing that knocks a
knight's armour off. The theme's zombies are **zombie knights**: little knights in rusty
armour, green faces, red eyes, with the usual zombie rule. The villain is **Vana Toomas**, the
knight from the top of the town hall spire, with his flag.

**Level 1: Tallinna vanalinn (the Old Town).** The original level, rebuilt by hand: cobbled
streets, steps, a pit, `?` blocks, the rainbow block, dolls and cars. Then the town gate: a
tower too tall to jump, its doorway bricked up with cracked stone, and the big head station in
front of it. The bow comes back for the first two zombie knights; a second tower with a cannon
on top; Vana Toomas runs off with the sibling at the end.

**Level 2: Tornid (the Towers).** Springs up, walls down. A spring throws you onto the town
wall, where a cracked wall bars the wall walk; a tower whose floors are cracked, so a big head
drops you through them one by one; a target hidden behind a cracked wall, opening a gate when
you smash the wall and shoot it; zombie knights in the passage under the wall. At the end a
spring up the last tower, and Toomas leaps away from its top.

**Level 3: Raekoja plats (Town Hall Square).** Harder together: zombie knights guarding a
cracked wall (shoot them, then smash it); a cracked wall high up that only a spring reaches,
with a gate's target behind it; cannons on the walls. Then the square: the door shuts behind
you, and Vana Toomas.

**The boss: Vana Toomas.** A knight three tiles tall in helmet, breastplate and shield, with his
flag on a pole, marching up and down the square; the sibling in a cage on the town hall steps.

- His armour turns everything without a big head: a stomp bounces you off with a clang,
  walking into him pushes you back, arrows clang off. No harm done: he is the one to catch.
- With a big head (two big head stations in the square, coming back as always), **land on
  him, or run into his back**: a piece of armour flies off. His shield covers his front, so
  running into him from the front just clangs. Three pieces: helmet, breastplate, shield.
- He stops now and then, raises his flag pole and stamps it down: a shockwave rolls along the
  ground both ways. Jump it. Slow and rare on Super Easy. While the pole is up he is braced,
  and even a big head clangs off: the moment to land on him is while he marches.
- After each hit he throws you off his helmet to the side, staggers well away, calls two zombie
  knights (never more than two up; a bow station is in the square), and stamps as soon as he
  recovers; then he marches faster, stamping more often. (Without the stagger and the brace,
  in the browser, bouncing on his helmet won the fight in seconds.)
- With the shield gone he sits down in his underclothes, beaten; the cage opens.

## Palermo, in detail (batch 4)

The tool is the **chicken ray**: eight shots from a station, fired in place of arrows while any
are left, and whatever it hits becomes a chicken. The theme's zombies are **zombie cooks**:
little cooks in tall white hats gone grey, green faces, red eyes, the usual zombie rule. The
villain is **Pitsakokk, the Pizza Chef**, guarded by **giants**.

Two rules change on the hand-built levels, so the tool reads plainly to a child:

- **A chicken never hurts.** What a ray makes is a chicken that clucks about and does no harm;
  jump on it for points. (A chicken hurt like any enemy, so a ray made a harmless zombie
  dangerous.) The six original levels keep the original rule.
- **Giants never hurt either; they are in the way.** A giant is three tiles tall with his arms
  folded: you cannot walk through him, a stomp bounces you off, an arrow bounces off, and a
  chicken ray makes a chicken of him. Giants stand where a jump cannot get over them: in an
  archway, under a roof, in a lane. (The giant was a dino drawn big, and landing on him hurt.)

**Level 1: Palermo väljak (the Piazza).** The original level, rebuilt by hand: the sunny
piazza, arches and balconies, pits, `?` blocks, the rainbow block, dolls, cars, a bouncer. The
chicken ray station, and just after it two zombie cooks out in the open; then the first giant,
in a low archway, a station in front of him. At the end the Pizza Chef runs off with the sibling.

**Level 2: Turg (the Market).** Giants open things. Market stalls to climb; a giant in a lane
between stalls; a giant standing in front of a target, so the ray goes to him first and then
to the target, which lifts a gate; zombie cooks in a covered passage; a spring up onto the
market roofs and a giant up there under the awning. The Chef runs off over the roofs.

**Level 3: Katedraal (the Cathedral).** Harder together: the cathedral steps; two giants in a
row in a portico, so two rays; zombie cooks in front of a giant; a gate that needs two targets,
one behind a giant. Then the square in front of the cathedral: the door shuts behind you, and
the Pizza Chef.

**The boss: Pitsakokk.** A cook two tiles wide and three tall in a white hat and apron, with a
moustache, at his pizza oven; the sibling in a cage on the cathedral steps. The square is
small enough to keep him in view. Two chicken ray
stations in the square, coming back as always.

- He tosses pizzas: each flies in an arc to where the hero stands and splats. Move and it
  misses; an arrow or a ray knocks one out of the air. Slow and rare on Super Easy.
- He is **guarded by giants**, one in the first round and two after, always between the hero
  and him (never behind the hero: in the browser two came down either side of a hero and boxed
  them in). While any giant stands, or one he called has not come yet, he bats a ray away with
  his pizza peel; a ray makes a chicken of the giant.
- With no giant left, **ray him: he turns into a big chicken in a chef's hat** and runs about
  the square, clucking, for a few seconds (ten on Super Easy, fewer on Hard). **Jump on the
  chicken**: a hit. He pops back into himself, sits dazed, walks back to his oven and calls
  more giants. Not caught in time, he pops back unhurt, and you ray him again.
- He never hurts by touch: walking into him pushes you off, a stomp on the cook bounces you
  off; only the pizzas hurt.
- Three hits. Then he flops over in a cloud of flour, his hat rolls off, the cage opens.

## Winter, in detail (batch 5)

The tools are **all four**, one level at a time as reminders, and the new thing is **ice**.
The theme's zombies are **zombie penguins**: the penguin, grey-green with red eyes, the usual
zombie rule. The villain is **the Yeti**.

- **Ice** is a floor you slide on: you speed up slowly, and let go and you keep sliding three or
  four tiles; turning round takes a moment. You can always jump off it. Ice reads as pale blue
  with a white shine.
- **Icy water** lies under the frozen lake: falling in is a fall, like a pit or lava.

**Level 1: Talvine imedemaa (Winter Wonderland).** The original level, rebuilt: the snow field,
penguins and ice bats, `?` blocks, the rainbow block. The first ice: a long flat stretch to
feel the slide, then ice running up to a pit. The bow comes back for the first two zombie
penguins. At the end the Yeti runs off with the sibling.

**Level 2: Jäätunud järv (the Frozen Lake).** Ice floes on icy water, jump to jump, each long
enough to land on from a full jump and slide to a stop; a cliff of ice only a fart jump climbs; a cracked ice wall in front of a cave,
smashed with a big head; zombie penguins in the cave (the bow comes back). The Yeti leaps away
from the far shore.

**Level 3: Jääloss (the Ice Palace).** All of it together: a giant in the palace gate (the chicken
ray); a target behind cracked ice that opens a gate (big head, then bow); an icy hall with zombie
penguins; a fart up an ice wall. Then the throne room: the door shuts behind you, and the Yeti.

**The boss: the Yeti.** A big white furry beast, two and a half tiles tall, in a throne room with
a ceiling hung with icicles; the sibling in a cage on an ice dais at the far end.

- He roars and paws the floor (a long warning on Super Easy), then **charges** at the hero, and
  cannot stop until he slams into a wall. The charge hurts, from the front only: **jump over
  him**. Every plain jump clears him on every difficulty, best running at him; on Super Easy
  even standing still. His head throws a hero off, and he never charges into the wall at his
  back.
- The slam shakes the room: **icicles fall** from the ceiling, each shaking first with its
  shadow on the floor. An arrow breaks one. Few and slow on Super Easy.
- After the slam he **sits dizzy**, stars round his head: **jump on his head** for a hit. The
  spell is always long enough to run the whole room to him. Not in time, he shakes it off and
  roars again.
- He never hurts outside a charge: walking into him pushes you off.
- After each hit he calls two zombie penguins (a bow station in the room), and charges faster,
  bringing down more icicles.
- Three hits and he flops down on the ice, beaten; the cage opens.

## Toy Castle, in detail (batch 6)

The last theme: **everything** comes back, and the boss is the T-Rex, with his new fight. The
theme's zombies are **zombie toy soldiers**: little tin soldiers in tall black hats, gone
green, red eyes, the usual zombie rule. The T-Rex carries the sibling through the chases.

**Level 1: Mängulossi (Toy Castle).** The original level rebuilt: toy-block steps, `?` blocks,
the rainbow block, dolls, cars, bats. The bow and the first zombie toy soldiers; a moat with
the drawbridge up (shoot its target and the planks come down); the castle gate, a portcullis
with a target of its own. At the end the T-Rex runs off with the sibling.

**Level 2: Lossisaalid (the Castle Halls).** A spring up onto a gallery; a cracked wall (big
head); a wall only a fart climbs; a giant in a corridor (chicken ray); zombie soldiers. The
T-Rex runs off down the last hall.

**Level 3: Troonisaal (the Throne Room).** Every tool in turn, harder together: a gate with two
targets, zombie soldiers in front of a giant, a cracked tower door and a wall only a fart gets
over. Then the throne room: the door shuts behind you, and the T-Rex.

**The boss: the T-Rex.** Four and a half tiles long, walking up and down the throne room, the
sibling in a cage on a dais. Four stations in the room, one for each tool. A bubble over his
head shows the tool the next hit needs, and that station glows. His touch never hurts: you
bounce off or are pushed off. Now and then he stops in the middle of the room and roars,
breathing a fireball at the hero (an arrow knocks it out; slow and rare on Super Easy).

1. **Bow.** A big toy block hangs from the ceiling over the middle of the room, on a rope tied
   to a target by the door, with room to stand on either side of it. Shoot the target and the block drops: on his head, a hit. Missed,
   it smashes on the floor and a new one is lowered.
2. **Fart.** Only a fart jump lands hard enough: jump on his head with the fart on, a hit.
   Anything else bounces off.
3. **Big head.** He wears a crown: touch him with a big head and it smashes, a hit.
4. **Chicken ray.** He calls two little zombie dinos. Ray them into chickens; with none left,
   ray him: he shrinks into a tiny toy T-Rex. The cage opens, and the game is won.

## The final boss: the T-Rex

Today's sprite has both eyes facing front on a thin, upright body: it reads as an owl or a
kangaroo more than a dinosaur. It is redrawn as a T-Rex in profile: a long head with a big jaw
and teeth, a small eye, two tiny arms, thick legs and a long tail held out behind. Its four
poses keep their meaning (idle, walk, charge, roar), the roar opening the jaw wide.

**The new look comes in batch 1**, on today's fight, so the children see it straight away. **Its
new fight comes with Toy Castle's batch:** the final boss asks for every tool once — shoot the
targets that drop toy blocks on its head, fart-jump onto its head when it lowers it to roar,
smash the crown it wears with a big head, chicken-ray the little dinos it calls. Its arena keeps
it on screen (today it can stand half above the top edge, over the score). Built in batch 6: see "Toy Castle, in detail".

## Zombies

The guards become zombies everywhere: green-grey skin and dark clothes on the theme's own
walker (a zombie doll in Doll Garden, a zombie dino in Dinosaur Canyon, ...), a shuffling walk
towards you when you are close, and a low groan. The rule the child already knows stays: you
cannot stomp a zombie (you bounce off), it pushes you back and never hurts you, and an arrow
puts it down. They are not tinted red any more: a zombie palette replaces the tint.

## How a level ends

A level says how it ends:

- **chase** (levels 1-2): the villain waits at the end with the sibling, and runs off when the
  hero gets close. The overlay says "Follow them!". The cutscene shows the villain running
  with the sibling and the hero after them, into the next level.
- **boss** (level 3): the boss holds the sibling in a cage; beaten, the cage opens; walking in is
  the rescue, with today's "is safe!" overlay. The cutscene is today's snatch by the next
  theme's villain.
- **rescue**: today's ending, for the levels not yet reworked.

Each theme's villain is its boss, so the one carrying the sibling through Doll Garden is the
Doll Queen. Until a theme is reworked it keeps today's kidnapper. With every theme reworked (2026-11-23), the snatch
between themes is always the next theme's villain: the Pterodactyl takes the sibling into
Dinosaur Canyon, Vana Toomas into Tallinn, and so on to the T-Rex into Toy Castle, the
Pterodactyl with the sibling hanging below her.

## What gets built (batch 1)

- **Themes** as data: name, colours, hills, music, villain, zombie kind. Stages point at a theme.
- **Endings** on the level record (chase, boss, rescue), the chase ending in the world, the
  overlay and the cutscene.
- **Zombies:** the guard rule with a zombie palette per kind, a shuffle towards the hero, and a
  groan (a new synth sound).
- **A boss framework** for hand-built levels: a boss is pure rules in `src/game/bosses/` plus a
  Phaser view, with a door that shuts behind you and a cage. The Doll Queen first. Today's
  T-Rex fight stays as it is until Toy Castle's batch moves it onto this framework.
- **Multi-target gates:** a gate opens once every target that owns it is hit.
- **Doll Garden's three levels** in the run in place of today's Doll Garden and Doll Garden II.
- **The T-Rex's new look**, on today's fight.
- The original Doll Garden record stays in the code only for the tests that pin it to the
  original game, until cutover.

## Testing

As before: the rules unit-tested (the boss's whole fight: shield, target, dizzy, hit, three
hits, defeat, cage; chase endings; multi-target gates; zombies), every obstacle checked against
the jump on all four difficulties, both heroes; then each level played in the browser with
real physics on Super Easy and Hard, which is where most of the real problems were found last
time; then the child.

## Out of scope for batch 1

The other five themes' levels and bosses; ice; the T-Rex's new fight. Saving progress.
